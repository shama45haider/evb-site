The Formula

How the Per-Gram Price Is Built

Silver is quoted per troy ounce. To get a price per gram, divide spot by 31.1035, then multiply by the purity and the weight in grams.

At $52.00 an ounce, pure silver is about $1.67 a gram and sterling is about $1.55. A 100-gram sterling bracelet holds 92.5 grams of silver, roughly $154.64 in melt value at that spot.

Spot moves every trading day, so no page can print a permanent number. The calculator does the arithmetic with today’s.

Before You Weigh

What Throws the Number Off

Plated pieces
EPNS, silverplate, “quadruple plate” and most pieces marked Sheffield carry a thin skin of silver over base metal. Their melt value is close to nothing. Nickel silver and German silver contain no silver at all.
Weighted pieces
Candlesticks, knife handles and some trophies are filled with pitch, resin or cement. That weight isn’t silver, so the scale overstates them. More on the flatware page.
Troy vs. kitchen ounces
A troy ounce is 31.1 grams. The ounce on a kitchen scale is 28.35. Weigh in grams to avoid mixing them up.
Stones and other parts
Stones, steel springs and cord aren’t silver and don’t belong in the weight.

FAQ

Silver Price Questions

Quick answers about silver prices and melt value.

How much is a gram of sterling silver worth?
Divide the spot price per troy ounce by 31.1035 and multiply by 0.925. At $52 an ounce that’s about $1.55 a gram. The calculator above uses the live price.
What does 925 mean on silver?
It means 925 parts per thousand are silver: 92.5 percent, the sterling standard. The rest is usually copper. Our stamp guide covers the other marks.
Is a troy ounce the same as a regular ounce?
No. Precious metals are priced in troy ounces of 31.1 grams. A regular ounce is 28.35 grams, so mixing them up understates silver by about 9 percent.
Is silver-plated jewelry or flatware worth anything for melt?
Very little. Plating is a thin layer over base metal, so there isn’t enough silver to recover. Plated pieces sell as decor, if at all.
Why won’t anyone pay the full melt value?
The buyer has to refine the metal, carry the risk of the price moving and run a business. Every buyer pays below melt. Compare buyers on how close they come and whether they show you weight, purity and spot.
Where does the live price come from?
The spot field loads from a public market data feed when the page opens. If it can’t load, type in today’s price and the calculator works the same.
