East Village Resale Index

Fall 2026 edition · August 1 to November 20, 2026

We run a buy counter at 39 Avenue A. Every piece we buy gets logged: what it is, the brand and what we paid. This index adds those records up. The first edition covers 1,211 purchases and 1,556 items, and it shows that New York cashes in its closet long before its jewelry box.

Puffer season arrives in October

  1. 6Aug
  2. 19Sep
  3. 54Oct
  4. 25Nov1st to 20th

Jackets and coats bought per month. November is shown lighter because it covers 20 days.

Jacket and coat purchases went from 6 in August to 19 in September and 54 in October, nine times the August count. By November 20 we had bought 25 more.

In October and November the jacket brands we bought most were Moose Knuckles (10), Moncler (9) and Mackage (5). Across the whole period a typical Moncler got $200, Moose Knuckles $150 and Mackage about $170.

Collectibles moved too: 15 came in during October, against 3 in each of August and September, led by Pokémon cards.

Fridays close at 4 PM, so Friday is the slowest day but the busiest hour for hour: about 3.3 purchases an hour, against 2.8 on a Monday.

How we count

  • Every completed purchase logged in our point-of-sale system from August 1 to November 20, 2026, New York time: 1,211 purchases, 1,556 items, 91 days open.
  • Only what we bought from the public. Our own sales, trades and cancelled tickets are left out.
  • Amounts are what we paid per item, rounded to $5. We use medians, not averages, so one big buy can’t skew a row.
  • Categories and brands are what staff entered at the counter. We merged obvious duplicates, such as Air Jordan into Jordan. About 30% of items have no brand logged.
  • No seller information is used or published.

Questions about the index

Quick answers about the numbers and how to use them.

What does East Village Buyers pay for sneakers?
From August to November 2026 the typical pair got $100, and the middle half of pairs got between $50 and $160. Jordans typically got $70 and Nike $80. The model, size and condition move the number most, so text photos of yours for an actual offer.
Which brand gets the most per piece?
Chrome Hearts: a typical piece got $350, the most of any brand we bought 15 times or more. Louis Vuitton and Moncler came next at $200.
Is this all of New York?
No. It is one shop: everything people sold to East Village Buyers at 39 Avenue A. It shows what walks into a busy Manhattan buy counter, not the whole resale market.
Can I use these numbers in an article?
Yes. Quote or chart any figure with a link to this page. The data is under a Creative Commons Attribution license, and the CSV is free to download.
