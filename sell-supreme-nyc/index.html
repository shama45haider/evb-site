What We Buy

Box Logos, Outerwear, Collabs, Accessories

Supreme opened on Lafayette Street in 1994 and has made a lot of product since. Not all of it has resale value. Box logos, outerwear, the big collabs and older seasons carry it; plain logo basics from recent seasons usually bring modest offers.

Box Logo Hoodies & Tees

The box logo is stitched on hoodies and crewnecks and printed on tees. Color and season decide most of the number, then how clean the logo, cuffs and collar are. Our box logo guide covers which years still pay.

Pricing

What Moves a Supreme Offer

Two Supreme hoodies on the same hanger can be far apart in value. Here is what decides it.

Season and graphic
We date a piece by its tags, its graphic and the season it dropped. Older seasons and graphics people still look for bring more than recent general releases.
Box logo color
On a box logo, color changes the number more than anything except condition. Some colors from the same season sell for far more than others.
Print and logo condition
Cracked or peeling prints, a fuzzy or picked box logo and faded fabric all come off the offer. Our guide on stains, fading and cracking goes through it.
Collar, cuffs and pits
Stretched collars, frayed cuffs and pit stains are the wear that shows first on hoodies and tees, and the first places we look.
Size
Medium and Large move fastest. Small and XL sell too, with demand that depends on the piece.
Cut tags and alterations
A cut neck tag makes a piece harder to authenticate and lowers the offer. It doesn't always stop a sale; see how tag removal affects value.

Not sure what season you have? Text photos of the front, the neck tag and the wash tag to 917-608-8939.

Authentication

How We Check Supreme

Supreme is one of the most faked brands there is, and good fakes get the box logo close. We look at the whole piece, in front of you, and tell you what we see.

  • Neck tag and wash tag: font, spacing, stitching and how the tags sit together
  • The Made in Canada tag under the neck label on box logo hoodies and crewnecks
  • Box logo letter shapes and spacing, and the stitching around the patch
  • Fabric weight, ribbing and seams
  • Collab pieces: both brands' labels and details done right, and Louis Vuitton x Supreme leather checked like any Louis Vuitton
  • Whether the graphic matches a real release from that season

Get Ready

Before You Bring It In

Clean is good. Wash on cold, inside out, and hang it to dry; dryer heat is what cracks prints. Don't cut tags or try to fix a print.

  • Bring receipts or order emails if you have them; they help but aren't required
  • Keep collab pieces complete: dust bags, boxes, pouches and straps
  • Leave sealed packs and accessories in their packaging
  • Bring a valid government-issued ID; New York law requires it for every sale

FAQ

Supreme Questions

What sellers ask us most at the counter.

Do you buy Supreme that isn't a box logo?
Yes. Jackets, collabs, graphic hoodies and tees, bags and accessories all sell. Plain logo basics from recent seasons usually bring modest offers, so text a photo first if you're not sure.
Do you buy worn Supreme?
Yes. Worn box logos still sell. The offer reflects what we see: print cracking, collar stretch, pit stains, fading and pilling.
Do you buy Louis Vuitton x Supreme?
Yes. We authenticate the leather pieces the same way we check any Louis Vuitton. Bring the dust bag, box and receipt if you have them; they help but aren't required.
Do you buy Supreme Nike Dunks and other sneakers?
Yes. Supreme SB Dunks and Air Force 1s are covered on our Nike page, and the rest on our sneakers page.
What if my Supreme doesn't pass authentication?
We don't buy fakes or replicas. If a piece doesn't pass, we show you what we saw and why.
Can I get a quote before coming in?
Yes. Text photos of the front, back, neck tag and wash tag to 917-608-8939 for a rough range. Final offers are made in person.
