What We Buy

Alhambra, Perlée and Frivole

Alhambra, the four-leaf clover, is Van Cleef's best-known line. It's made in 18k gold, so a genuine piece has real metal in it, and the design has steady demand on top of that. It's also one of the most copied designs in jewelry, so every piece gets checked closely.

Vintage Alhambra

The standard-size clover, introduced in 1968, with a beaded gold border around each stone. Pendants, earrings, rings, the 5-motif bracelet and the 10- and 20-motif necklaces.

  • 10 motifs
  • 20 motifs

Sweet, Magic & Byzantine Alhambra

Sweet Alhambra is the smallest clover. Magic Alhambra is larger, and its long necklaces often mix motif sizes. Byzantine Alhambra uses openwork gold motifs. Each is priced on its own.

Pricing

What Moves a Van Cleef Offer

Two Alhambra pieces that look alike can be far apart in value. Here is what decides it.

Size and motif count
A Sweet pendant, a 5-motif bracelet and a 20-motif necklace hold very different amounts of gold and stone. We price the exact piece, not the line.
Stone and gold color
Which stone, which gold and whether it was a limited combination all change the number. Diamond pavé pieces carry their stones on top of the gold.
Stone condition
Mother-of-pearl, malachite and turquoise are soft stones and chip or scratch with wear. Chipped, cracked or dull stones lower the offer, and replacement stones not set by Van Cleef lower it more.
Stamps and serial
Genuine pieces carry the VCA or Van Cleef & Arpels signature, a gold mark (750 or Au750) and a serial number, usually near the clasp on bracelets and necklaces.
Repairs and alterations
Shortened chains, clasps that aren't Van Cleef and motifs that have been resoldered lower the value. Tell us about any work done on the piece.
Certificate and box
The certificate and receipt help, especially on larger pieces. None of it is required; a piece that passes authentication sells without it.

Not sure what you have? Count the motifs and look near the clasp for the stamps and serial. Text photos of both to 917-608-8939.

Authentication

How We Check Van Cleef & Arpels

Fake Alhambra is everywhere, and the stamps and serial format get copied too. Some fakes are even made in real gold, so a gold test alone doesn't settle it. We look at the whole piece, in front of you, and tell you what we see.

  • The beading around each motif: even, round and tight to the stone
  • How each stone is cut and how it sits inside its gold frame
  • The signature, gold mark and serial number: font, depth and placement
  • The clasp and the chain between the motifs
  • Weight, and a metal test on the gold

Get Ready

Before You Bring It In

Don't have it shortened, repaired or polished before you sell. Bring it as it is.

  • Bring the certificate, box and receipt if you have them
  • Bring both earrings of a pair and every piece of a set
  • Text photos of the clasp and the back of a motif first if you're coming from far away
  • Bring a valid government-issued ID; New York law requires it for every sale

FAQ

Van Cleef & Arpels Questions

What sellers ask us most at the counter.

Do you buy Van Cleef without the certificate?
Yes. The certificate and receipt help, but we authenticate the piece itself, and a genuine piece sells without them.
Is the stamp enough to prove my Alhambra is real?
No. Fakes copy the VCA stamp and the serial format. The stamp is one check among several; the beading, the stones, the clasp and the weight have to agree with it.
Do you buy Alhambra with a chipped or cracked stone?
Yes. It's still 18k gold and still Van Cleef. Damage to the stone lowers the offer, and we'll show you how it changes the number.
What if my piece turns out to be fake?
We'll tell you directly what we're seeing and why. If it's made of real gold, that metal still has value; it just won't carry the Van Cleef premium.
Can I trade Van Cleef toward something in your store?
Yes. You can put the value of your piece toward store stock instead of taking payment.
Can I get a quote before coming in?
Yes. Text photos of the front, the back and the clasp with its stamps to 917-608-8939 for a rough range. Final offers are made in person.
