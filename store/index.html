Why you can trust the listing

Authentication is the whole business

We buy for cash across the counter. That only works if we are right about what something is — so the same testing that protects us on the buy protects you on the sale.

Metals

XRF analyzer and acid testing on every piece. Gold and silver are weighed in front of you and priced against live spot.

Watches

Movement timed on a machine, caseback opened where appropriate, serials and papers checked against reference data.

Sneakers & streetwear

Stitch density, tagging, box labels and materials. The tells that fakes get wrong are the ones we look at first.

Electronics

Wiped to factory, Activation Lock removed, IMEI checked against the carrier blacklist before anything is listed.
