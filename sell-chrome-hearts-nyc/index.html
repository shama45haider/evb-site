Pricing

What Moves a Chrome Hearts Offer

Two cross rings that look alike can be far apart in value. Here is what decides it.

Silver weight and design
Every silver piece starts with its weight in .925 silver. The design and how in-demand it is decide how far above that the offer goes.
22k gold
Gold pieces are worth at least their gold, and 22k holds more gold per gram than 14k or 18k. The brand premium comes on top.
Wear and alterations
The dark finish in the recesses is part of the design, and normal wear is fine. Deep dents, cracked solder and rings resized by someone other than Chrome Hearts lower the offer.
Clothing condition
Cracked or faded prints, stains, stretched cuffs and cut tags lower the number. Size matters too, model by model.
Limited pieces
Collaborations, Matty Boy pieces and limited releases usually bring more than everyday basics in the same category.
Receipt and packaging
A Chrome Hearts receipt, bag and pouch are nice to have, but they don't prove a piece is real on their own. We check the piece itself.

Not sure what you have? Look for the Chrome Hearts mark and the .925 or 22K stamp. Text photos of the piece and the stamps to 917-608-8939.

Authentication

How We Check Chrome Hearts

Fakes copy the logos and stamps well, so we check the whole piece, in front of you, and tell you what we see. Our Chrome Hearts authentication guide covers the tells in more detail.

  • The Chrome Hearts mark and the .925 or 22K stamp: font, depth and spacing
  • Casting detail inside the crosses and on the underside of rings and pendants
  • Weight, and a metal test so silver and gold are confirmed, not assumed
  • On clothing: print quality, neck and wash tags, and stitching
  • On eyewear and leather: hinges, markings, stitching and the silver hardware

Get Ready

Before You Bring It In

Don't polish the silver or have anything resized before you sell. Bring it as it is.

  • Bring the receipt, bag and pouch if you have them
  • Bring clothing clean and folded, with every tag still on
  • Bring eyewear with its case and any extra parts
  • Bring a valid government-issued ID; New York law requires it for every sale

FAQ

Chrome Hearts Questions

What sellers ask us most at the counter.

Do you buy Chrome Hearts without a receipt?
Yes. A receipt is nice to have, but it doesn't prove a piece is real on its own. We authenticate the piece itself, and a genuine piece sells without one.
Do you buy worn Chrome Hearts?
Yes. Normal wear on silver is expected. Heavy damage, resizing and stretched or faded clothing lower the offer, but they don't stop a sale.
Is Chrome Hearts gold really 22k?
Chrome Hearts gold pieces are usually 22k and stamped 22K. We test the metal at the counter, since a stamp alone isn't proof.
Do you buy Chrome Hearts clothing and hats?
Yes. Hoodies, tees, long sleeves, sweatpants, trucker hats and beanies. Condition and size decide most of the offer.
Can I trade Chrome Hearts toward something in your store?
Yes. You can put the value of your piece toward store stock instead of taking payment.
Can I get a quote before coming in?
Yes. Text photos of the piece, the stamps and any tags to 917-608-8939 for a rough range. Final offers are made in person.
