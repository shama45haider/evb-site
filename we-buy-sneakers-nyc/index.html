How We Authenticate

How Do We Authenticate Sneakers?

Every pair is inspected in front of you at the counter.

Fake sneakers flood the market. We check brand-specific details before making any offer:

  • Materials & Build — leather quality, stitching, glue lines, midsole foam
  • Labels & Tags — size tag fonts, UPC codes, production date formatting
  • Box & Packaging — label matching, tissue paper, lace bags when present
  • Brand Markers — Nike swoosh shape, Jordan wings logo, Yeezy boost pellets
  • Cross-Reference — compared against retail releases and resale databases

If it doesn't pass, we'll tell you why. If it does, you get a cash offer on the spot.

Valuation

How Much Are Your Sneakers Worth?

The honest answer is that two pairs of the same shoe can be worth very different numbers. Five things decide where yours lands — here's how each one moves the offer.

1Model & Colorway

This matters more than anything else. Limited releases and collabs — Travis Scott, Fragment, Off-White, CLOT — hold or gain value for years. General releases that sat on shelves drop fast no matter how clean they are. OG colorways on the Jordan 1, 3, 4, and 11 are consistently the strongest performers we see.

2Condition

Deadstock (never worn, with the box) sits at the top. From there, offers step down with toe creasing, sole wear, heel drag, and yellowing on icy or clear outsoles. Light wear on a rare pair often still beats deadstock on a common one — rarity outruns condition more often than people expect.

3Size

Men's 8 through 12 move fastest in New York, so those sizes carry the strongest offers. Very small and very large sizes take longer to resell, which shows up in the number — but we still buy them.

4Box & Extras

The original box with a matching size label, spare laces, hang tags, and any special packaging all add to the offer. None of it is required — we buy plenty of pairs with no box — but having it consistently pays more.

5Current Demand

Sneaker resale moves week to week. A retro that's about to be re-released usually softens; a pair getting attention from a drop or a player moment can spike. We price against what the market is actually doing the day you walk in, not a stale list.

6Authenticity

Everything above only matters once a pair verifies as authentic. We authenticate in front of you before quoting anything — and if something doesn't pass, we'll show you exactly what we're seeing rather than just declining.

Want a number before you come in? Text clear photos of the shoes, the size tag, and the box label to 917-608-8939 and we'll give you a rough range the same day.
