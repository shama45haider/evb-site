What We Buy

Cable, Albion, Men's and Gold

Most David Yurman is sterling silver with gold accents. The silver in a bracelet is worth little next to its retail price, so resale on silver pieces is a fraction of what they cost new. Gold and diamond pieces hold more, because the metal and stones are worth real money on their own.

Cable Classics

The twisted cable bracelet, introduced in 1983, with gold end caps that are often set with a stone or pearl. Most are sterling silver with 14k or 18k gold accents; all-gold versions exist too and are worth much more.

  • Cable bracelet
  • Cable cuff
  • Cable rings

Albion

A cushion-shaped color stone in a cable frame: blue topaz, amethyst, citrine and others, in rings, earrings and pendants, some with a pavé diamond border. Color stones like these add less than people expect; diamonds add more.

  • Rings
  • Earrings
  • Pendants

Men's Chains, Rings & Bracelets

Curb and box chains, signet rings, Streamline, Chevron, Armory and the Spiritual Beads bracelets. Mostly sterling silver; the heavy chains carry more silver than most David Yurman pieces.

  • Curb chains
  • Signet rings
  • Spiritual Beads

Gold & Diamond Pieces

All-gold cable bracelets, pavé diamond pieces and gold bands. Their gold and diamonds put a floor under the offer, so they keep much more of their value than silver.

  • 18k gold
  • Pavé diamonds
  • Bands

Pricing

What Moves a David Yurman Offer

We'd rather you hear this at home than at the counter. Here is what decides the number.

Silver or gold
The stamp tells most of the story: 925 is sterling silver, 585 is 14k gold, 750 is 18k. A silver cable bracelet with gold end caps is mostly silver by weight.
Retail vs. resale
Most of a silver piece's retail price is design and brand, not metal. Used pieces sell for well below retail, and our offer follows the resale market, not the price tag.
Collection
Cable bracelets and men's chains have the steadiest demand. Older fashion pieces and lines that are no longer made bring less.
Stones
Blue topaz, amethyst and other color stones add little on resale. Diamonds add more, and pavé pieces are checked stone by stone.
Condition
A stretched or loose cable, a dented end cap or a weak clasp lowers the offer. Tarnish isn't a problem.
Box and pouch
The box, pouch and care card help a little. None of it is required, and a genuine piece without them sells.

Not sure what you have? The stamps are usually near the clasp, on an end cap or inside a ring. Text photos of them to 917-608-8939.

Authentication

How We Check David Yurman

Fake cable bracelets are common. We check the piece in front of you and tell you what we see.

  • The maker's mark: D.Y., DY or D. Yurman, usually with a © and the metal marks 925, 585 or 750
  • The cable: an even, tight twist from end to end with no gaps
  • A metal test on the end caps and accents, so plated gold doesn't pass as 14k or 18k
  • How stones are set, and the pavé on diamond pieces
  • Weight, and how the clasp opens and closes

Get Ready

Before You Bring It In

Light tarnish is fine. Don't polish or repair anything first; bring it as it is.

  • Bring the box, pouch and receipt if you have them
  • Bring both earrings of a pair and every piece of a set
  • Text photos first if you're bringing a large collection
  • Bring a valid government-issued ID; New York law requires it for every sale

FAQ

David Yurman Questions

What sellers ask us most at the counter.

Why is the offer so far below what I paid?
Most David Yurman is sterling silver, and most of the retail price is design and brand rather than metal. Used pieces sell for well below retail, and our offer follows that resale market. Gold and diamond pieces hold more of their value.
Do you buy David Yurman without the box?
Yes. We authenticate the piece itself. The box and pouch help a little, but a genuine piece sells without them.
Is my David Yurman silver or gold?
Check the stamp: 925 is sterling silver, 585 is 14k gold and 750 is 18k. Many pieces carry two stamps because they're silver with gold accents. We test the metal at the counter either way.
Do you buy men's David Yurman?
Yes. Chains, signet rings, bracelets and pendants. Heavier silver chains carry more metal, which helps the offer.
Do you buy damaged David Yurman?
Yes. A broken clasp or a stretched cable lowers the brand premium, but the silver and gold are still there and still count.
Can I get a quote before coming in?
Yes. Text photos of the piece and its stamps to 917-608-8939 for a rough range. Final offers are made in person.
