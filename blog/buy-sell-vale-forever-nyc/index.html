Buy & Sell Vale Forever in NYC: What Your Hoodies, Tees and Jorts Are Worth

Vale Forever is one of the most searched streetwear names of 2026, and pieces carrying its Valley Dreams graphics are selling fast on every resale app. If you've got a hoodie you stopped wearing, a tee from a drop you grabbed on release day, or jorts that never fit right, that's cash sitting in your closet. And if you've been trying to buy Vale Forever without getting burned by a fake "official" site, buying a piece you can hold in your hands is still the safest way to do it. Here's how both sides work at East Village Buyers on Avenue A.

Selling Vale Forever? Text clear photos (front, back and tags) to 917-608-8939 for a quote, or walk into 39 Avenue A. No appointment needed, cash the same day.

Buying Vale Forever? Check the online store or text us your size. Streetwear stock changes every week, and every piece is checked by hand before it's priced.

What Vale Forever Pieces We Buy

The photo above shows the range buyers want right now: a cream zip-up hoodie with distressed, all-over red graphics, a washed black Valley Dreams tee with a snarling bulldog print, and a white "Valley Dreams 199X" tee with a vintage-style portrait graphic. Washed fabrics, loud graphics and a '90s bootleg feel are the reason this label stands out in a feed full of plain logo tees, and the reason it resells.

We buy the full line, new or worn:

  • Hoodies and zip-ups, including the Dreams zip-up hoodie and distressed all-over-print zips
  • Graphic tees, including Valley Dreams tees and the Dream Crasher tee
  • Flannels, like the Reborn and Lemonhead flannels
  • Jorts and denim, like the Rainstorm V2 and Ice Cream jorts
  • Tracksuit sets, caps and bandanas, including the color-blocked sets with VSX branding (more in our Vale Forever brand guide)
  • Kids' sizes: the Valley Dreams kids' jerseys are part of the same line, not knockoffs

Is Vale Forever Worth Anything on Resale?

Yes. On the big resale platforms, popular Vale Forever tees, flannels and jorts keep selling months after their drops, which is the clearest sign a brand has real demand and not just a viral moment. The pieces that hold the most value are recent drops in clean condition, limited colorways, and anything that sold out fast on the brand's own site.

To be straight about how a shop offer compares: we pay less than a stranger online might eventually pay, because we take on the risk, the cleaning, the listing and the wait. In exchange you get cash today, no fees, nothing to ship, and no buyer opening a "not as described" claim two weeks later.

How to Get the Most Cash for Your Vale Forever

  1. Wash it inside out on cold and hang it to dry. Heat is what cracks graphics and shrinks fleece.
  2. Bring sets together. A matching tracksuit or hoodie-and-sweats set is worth more as a set than as two separate pieces.
  3. Keep the tags, receipts and bags. Proof of purchase helps, especially on recent drops.
  4. Text photos first. Send the front, the back, a close-up of the graphic, the neck tag and the wash tag. We'll give you a fast read before you make the trip.
  5. Bring more than one piece. We make one offer on the whole stack, and you can mix in sneakers, gold or electronics. Here's how selling several categories in one trip works.

Buying Vale Forever in NYC Without Getting Burned

Right now the biggest risk in buying Vale Forever isn't the clothes, it's the websites. Search the brand and you'll find a cluster of near-identical "official" stores using the same recycled product photos. Buying in person solves that: you see the actual piece, check the print and stitching yourself, and try it on before you pay.

Every streetwear piece on our racks has been inspected by hand before it's priced. If you don't see your size in the store, text us and we'll tell you what just came in. You can also trade: bring in something you're done wearing and put its value toward a piece on the rack.
