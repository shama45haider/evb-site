Buy & Sell SB Studios (Stainbandz) in NYC: Cash for Sweatsuits, Hoodies and Sets

SB Studios, also sold as Stainbandz, is a home-grown New York label, and its pieces are in demand right here in the city. Matching sweatsuits with embroidered logos, puff-print graphic sets and crop-top-and-shorts sets all sell, and sold-out drops keep people searching resale long after release. If you have SB Studios you're not wearing, here's how to turn it into cash at East Village Buyers. If you're trying to buy it, here's how to get the real thing.

Buying SB Studios? Check the online store or text us your size. Every piece is inspected by hand before it's priced.

The Stainbandz Sweatsuit

The photo above shows the brand's most-wanted basic: matching fleece hoodie-and-sweatpant sets in heather grey, royal blue and black. Each has a stitched "STAIN BANDZ" wordmark on the chest, a larger outlined splash logo beside it, and the same logos repeated on the leg of the sweats. They're simple and heavyweight, made to be worn as a full set, and styled here with Air Max 95s.

Beyond the sweatsuits, SB Studios is known for loud, color-forward graphic sets. Collections like "Seeing Stars," "American Optimist" and "Save the Planet" pair a graphic tee or crop top with matching shorts or sweats, often finished with puff-print lettering or rhinestones. For the backstory, read how SB Studios got so popular in NYC.

SB Studios and Stainbandz Pieces We Buy

  • Sweatsuits: hoodie and sweatpant sets in every colorway
  • Hoodies and zip-ups, including fleece and premium mohair-style pieces
  • Graphic tee and shorts sets from collections like Seeing Stars, American Optimist and Save the Planet
  • Crop top and shorts sets

Why Full Sets Get a Better Offer

People buy SB Studios for the matching look. A full set, both pieces in the same size and colorway and in similar condition, sells to the next buyer as-is. A hoodie on its own has to wait for someone who already owns the pants, or who will wear it with something else. That's why we pay more for the two pieces together than for each one separately. If you have both halves, bring both.

Spotting Real SB Studios Before You Buy or Sell

  • Embroidery: the Stainbandz wordmark and splash logo should be dense and clean-edged, with no loose threads or puckering around the stitching.
  • Logo proportions: letter spacing and the shape of the splash should match verified pieces. Copies often get the spacing slightly wrong.
  • Fleece weight: genuine sweatsuits are heavy and structured. Thin fleece that feels light is a warning sign.
  • Tags: check the neck and wash tags against a confirmed piece from the same collection.
  • Where it came from: the brand's own drops, NYC streetwear shops that carry it, or a reseller who can show you the actual piece. A stock photo is not proof.

Selling to a Shop vs. Selling Online

A sweatsuit is bulky to ship, and buyers online ask for measurements, close-ups and proof before they commit. Then the platform takes its cut. At East Village Buyers you walk in with the set and walk out with cash. Our offer is below what you might eventually get from a patient online sale, because we take on the risk, the cleaning and the wait, but there are no fees, no shipping and no returns.

How to Get the Most Cash for SB Studios

  1. Wash it inside out on cold and air-dry it. Dryer heat is what pills fleece, fades black and flattens puff print.
  2. Bring both halves of every set.
  3. De-pill gently with a fabric shaver if the fleece has light pilling. Don't cut or trim the logos.
  4. Text photos first: the front, back, logo close-ups and tags.
  5. Bring the whole stack. We'll make one offer on all of it, and you can add sneakers, jewelry or electronics to the same visit.
