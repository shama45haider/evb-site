Mixed Emotion Clothing: Where to Buy & Sell Rhinestone Hoodies and Tees in NYC

Mixed Emotion (you'll also see it listed as "Mixed Emotions") has gone from a niche name to one of the most searched streetwear labels of 2026. The rhinestone hoodies are everywhere, and the oversized tees with big front-and-back art are easy to spot from across the street. That attention is good news if you own some, because it sells. Here's how to get the most cash for Mixed Emotion clothing in NYC, and how to buy it without paying for a fake.

Buying Mixed Emotion? Check the online store or text us the piece and size you want. Every piece is checked by hand before it's priced.

The Mixed Emotion Look, and Why It Sells

The photo above sums up the brand. On the left, an oversized white tee with gothic "ME" lettering set in rhinestones over a painted cherub archer. On the right, a red tee with "EMOTION" in rhinestone graffiti letters over a scene of winged cherubs working under the hood of a car. Both carry a second full-size graphic across the back.

Classical art mixed with street culture, rhinestones and heavy, boxy blanks: that combination photographs well, stands out in a feed, and is exactly what keeps resale demand up after a drop sells out.

Rhinestones: The Biggest Factor in Your Offer

With most graphic streetwear, print cracking is the main worry. With Mixed Emotion, it's the stones. A missing rhinestone shows, especially inside a letter. When we look at a piece we check for gaps, dull or cloudy stones, and glue marks from DIY repairs.

  • Full, even coverage: top offer.
  • A few stones missing at the edges: a small deduction.
  • Gaps inside the lettering or bare patches: a bigger deduction, because buyers notice it right away.
  • Re-glued with craft-store stones: can cost more than leaving the gaps, because the size and sparkle won't match.

To keep the stones on: wash inside out, on cold and gentle, and never tumble dry. Heat is what loosens the adhesive.

Real vs. Fake Mixed Emotion: What to Check

A brand this hot gets copied fast. Whether you're buying or selling, these are the checks that matter:

  • Rhinestone layout: real pieces have tight, even spacing that follows the letter shapes. Fakes tend to be sparse or crooked, or use flat plastic stones with no sparkle.
  • Print quality: the cherub art should be sharp with smooth color. Pixelated, muddy or off-color graphics are a red flag.
  • Blank weight and fit: the brand uses heavy, boxy, oversized blanks. A thin, fitted tee is a warning sign.
  • Tags and labels: fonts, placement and stitching should match verified pieces from the same drop.
  • Seller photos: if a seller can only show stock photos and not the actual piece, walk away.

How to Get the Most Cash for Mixed Emotion

  1. Look the stones over first. If any loose ones are still attached, leave them for us to see. Don't glue on replacements.
  2. Wash it inside out on cold and hang dry so the piece looks its best when we see it.
  3. Bring hoodie and sweats together if you have the set.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it Mixed Emotion or Mixed Emotions?

Both spellings show up on resale listings for the same label. We buy it under either name.

Do you buy Mixed Emotion hoodies with missing rhinestones?

Yes. Missing stones lower the offer, more so when the gaps are inside the lettering, but we still buy genuine pieces. Don't glue on replacement stones before you sell.
