Top 5 Places to Sell Chrome Hearts in NYC

Chrome Hearts is one of the few brands where a used piece can sell for close to what it cost new. Retail prices have climbed for years, the pieces people want are hard to get, and the sterling silver and 22k gold carry real metal value underneath the name. It's also one of the most copied brands out there, so where you sell matters as much as what you're selling.

Here are the five best places to sell Chrome Hearts in New York, ranked by how fast you get paid, what comes out in fees, and how each one handles authentication.

#3

The RealReal

Luxury Consignment · Online

The RealReal works on consignment. You send in your pieces, they authenticate, photograph and list them, and you're paid a share after each one sells. It carries a large Chrome Hearts selection, so the shoppers are there. The cost is time and commission: you won't know your final number until a piece sells, and the consignment share comes out of the sale price.

Best for
Higher-value pieces when you can wait.
Trade-off
No money until it sells, and commission comes out of the price.
#4

Grailed

Online Marketplace · Peer-to-Peer

A lot of Chrome Hearts changes hands between collectors on Grailed. You set the price, shoot your own photos, answer offers, ship the piece and get paid after the sale, minus the platform fee and payment processing. The right buyer will pay well for a rare ring or a sought-after hoodie. Buyers are wary of fakes, so clear photos of the hallmarks, the inside of the band and the tags make the difference.

Best for
Rare pieces when you have time to sell them yourself.
Trade-off
You do the listing, messaging and shipping, and fees come out of the sale.

What Moves a Chrome Hearts Offer

  • Hallmarks. Sterling pieces are stamped .925 along with the Chrome Hearts marks, and gold pieces are usually stamped 22K. Clean, readable stamps make a piece easier to verify. Our Chrome Hearts authentication guide covers what fakes get wrong.
  • Weight. Heavier silver and 22k gold pieces carry more metal value before the brand is counted. 22k is about 91.7% gold, more than 14k or 18k.
  • The finish. Chrome Hearts silver is meant to look dark in the recesses. Leave it alone; a hard polish can soften the detail.
  • Clothing condition. Clean prints, intact tags and no stretching or fading bring the most on hoodies and tees.
  • Extras. The receipt, bag or box help, but you don't need them to sell at our counter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I sell Chrome Hearts in person in NYC?

East Village Buyers at 39 Avenue A in the East Village buys Chrome Hearts over the counter. We check each piece in front of you and pay the same visit. The other main options, Fashionphile, The RealReal, Grailed and Vestiaire Collective, mostly work online or by appointment.

Do I need the receipt to sell Chrome Hearts?

Not at East Village Buyers. We authenticate the piece itself: the hallmarks, construction, weight and finish. A receipt, bag or box can help, but you can sell without them.

Is broken Chrome Hearts jewelry worth selling?

Yes. Chrome Hearts silver is .925 sterling and gold pieces are usually 22k, so a bent ring or a snapped chain still has its metal value. How much of the brand premium survives depends on whether the piece can be repaired.

Can I sell Chrome Hearts clothing, or only jewelry?

Both. We buy horseshoe hoodies and zip-ups, tees, long sleeves, sweatpants, trucker hats, leather and eyewear as well as jewelry. Clean prints, intact tags and no fading bring the most.
