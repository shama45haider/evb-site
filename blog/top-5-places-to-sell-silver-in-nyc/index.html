Top 5 Places to Sell Silver in NYC

Silver turns up in more forms than gold does: a jar of old quarters, a chest of flatware, a Tiffany bracelet, a 10-ounce bar. Each of those can have a different best buyer, and picking the wrong one is how people leave money behind.

Before you go anywhere, check the marks. STERLING, 925 or an English lion passant means sterling, which is 92.5% silver. EPNS, A1, TRIPLE or a maker's name with no sterling mark usually means silverplate, a thin skin of silver over base metal with almost no melt value. US dimes, quarters and half dollars dated 1964 or earlier are 90% silver. Then pick your buyer from the five below.

#3

Manfra, Tordella & Brookes

Bullion & Coin Dealer · Diamond District

Manfra, Tordella & Brookes is a precious metals and coin firm on West 47th Street, and it's on the US Mint's list of authorized purchasers for bullion coins. #4

Replacements, Ltd.

Tableware Buyer · Mail-In from North Carolina

Replacements is a North Carolina company that buys and sells china, crystal and flatware so people can complete their patterns. Because it resells pieces by pattern rather than melting them, it may want flatware that a melt buyer can't pay much for, including some silverplate. Expect to ship the pieces and to have the final number set once they're inspected.

Best for
Flatware sold piece by piece, by pattern.
Trade-off
Shipping heavy pieces, and the price is set after inspection.
#5

Online Bullion Dealers (APMEX, JM Bullion)

Bullion Buyback · Mail-In

Large online bullion dealers such as APMEX and JM Bullion buy back bullion coins and bars at posted prices, which makes them easy to compare. You lock in a price, ship the metal insured, and get paid after it's received and checked. Heavy silver costs money to ship, and jewelry and flatware aren't what these buyback programs are set up for.

Best for
Bullion coins and bars with clear prices.
Trade-off
Shipping costs and a wait, and no jewelry or flatware.

Know What Kind of Silver You Have

  • Sterling jewelry (.925). Priced on weight times purity times the day's silver price, with a premium for designer pieces. More in is silver jewelry worth selling.
  • Sterling flatware. Solid sterling pays well. Weighted handles and knife blades hold less silver than they weigh. Our flatware page explains how we sort a set.
  • Pre-1965 US coins. Dimes, quarters and halves from 1964 and earlier are 90% silver, and one dollar of face value holds roughly 0.715 troy ounces. Kennedy halves from 1965 to 1970 are 40% silver. See selling silver coins and how we sort Kennedy halves.
  • Bullion (.999). Eagles, Maple Leafs, rounds and bars are priced close to the silver price. Proofs and graded coins can be worth more, like a proof American Silver Eagle.
  • Silverplate. Almost no melt value. A pattern buyer may still want it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I sell silver for cash in NYC?

East Village Buyers at 39 Avenue A buys sterling jewelry, flatware, coins and bars. We weigh and test it in front of you, price it off the live silver price and pay the same visit.

How is silver priced when you sell it?

By weight, purity and the day's silver price. Sterling is 92.5% silver, pre-1965 US coins are 90% and bullion is usually .999. Designer pieces and collectible coins can be worth more than their metal.

Is silverplate worth selling?

Usually not for its metal. Silverplate is a very thin layer of silver over base metal. A pattern buyer such as Replacements may still want pieces from a set people are trying to complete.

Should I take silver coins to a coin dealer or a silver buyer?

If you have common, worn pre-1965 coins, a silver buyer is quicker and pays on silver content. If you have proofs, graded coins, early dollars or better dates, get a coin dealer's opinion first.

Do I need to clean my silver before selling it?

No. Tarnish doesn't change the silver content, and cleaning coins can lower their collector value.
