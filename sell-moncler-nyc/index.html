Pricing

What Moves a Moncler Offer

With Moncler, most of the value is in the down and the shell. Here is what decides it.

Down and loft
Down that has clumped, gone flat or started leaking through the seams lowers the offer. We feel every panel.
Shell condition
Tears, burns, patched holes and shine worn off the nylon all come off the number.
The model
Known models like the Maya and Montclar, and Genius pieces, move fastest. Older or less common styles take longer to sell.
Labels and patches
The logo patch on the sleeve, the inside labels and the authenticity label should all be there. Missing or cut tags bring less.
Size
Moncler uses number sizes. The middle sizes, roughly 2 to 4, move fastest.
Extras
The garment bag, removable fur trims, spare buttons and receipts add to the offer.

Not sure which model you have? Text photos of the front, the sleeve patch and the inside labels to 917-608-8939.

Authentication

How We Check Moncler

Moncler is one of the most faked jackets out there. Newer pieces carry a label with a QR code and an NFC chip that links to Moncler's own check, but fakes copy those labels too, so we look at the whole jacket.

  • The authenticity label on newer pieces: QR code, printed code and NFC chip
  • Sleeve logo patch: shape, stitching and colors
  • Down fill: weight, loft and how evenly it sits in each panel
  • Zippers, snaps and buttons, and how they are marked
  • Inside labels and care tags: font, spacing and print

Get Ready

Before You Bring It In

Don't wash a down jacket at home right before selling; it can clump the fill. Bring it clean and fully dry.

  • Bring the garment bag, removable fur trims and spare buttons
  • Bring receipts or order emails if you have them
  • Bring a valid government-issued ID; New York law requires it for every sale

FAQ

Moncler Questions

What sellers ask us most at the counter.

Do you buy worn Moncler?
Yes. The offer depends on the down, the shell and the labels. A worn jacket with full down and a clean shell sells; tears, burns and flat fill bring less.
Do you buy older Moncler without a QR label?
Yes. Older jackets were made before the QR and NFC labels, so we check the construction, the down and the labels instead.
Do you buy Moncler Genius pieces?
Yes. Genius collabs sell well when they're complete. Bring every removable piece and anything that came with the jacket.
Do you buy Moncler hoodies and knitwear?
Yes. Logo hoodies, knits, polos and tees sell, though down jackets and vests bring the most.
How do I know if my Moncler is real?
Bring it in and we'll check it in front of you. We don't buy fakes or replicas, and if a piece doesn't pass we show you why.
Can I get a quote before coming in?
Yes. Text photos of the front, back, sleeve patch and inside labels to 917-608-8939 for a rough range. Final offers are made in person.
