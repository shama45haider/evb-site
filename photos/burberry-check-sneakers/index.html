Selling Burberry Check Sneakers in NYC

A Burberry sneaker in the brand's Vintage Check, with a logo strap across the laces, at our counter on 39 Avenue A. The check is one of the most recognized patterns in fashion, and Burberry sneakers in it sell steadily. We price them in person and pay the same day.

What We Check on Burberry Sneakers

Fabric sneakers live or die on how clean the fabric is.

The check fabric

The check panels are fabric, which picks up dirt and stains faster than leather. Clean fabric is the biggest factor in the price.

Soles and leather

We look at the white sole for scuffs and yellowing, and at the leather panels for creasing and scratches.

Logo details

We check the Burberry logo on the strap, the size label inside and the stitching.

Box and dust bag

The original box and dust bag help resale.

Before You Bring Them In

Spot-clean the check with a soft brush and a little water, and don't put them in the washing machine. Bring both shoes, the box and dust bag, and a government-issued photo ID.
