The Formula

How the Per-Gram Price Is Built

Divide the spot price per troy ounce by 31.1035, then multiply by the purity and the weight in grams.

At $1,540.00 an ounce, pure platinum is about $49.51 a gram and PT950 is about $47.04. A 10-gram PT950 wedding band holds 9.5 grams of platinum, roughly $470.37 in melt value at that spot.

Older American pieces are often stamped IRIDPLAT or 10% IRID PLAT: 90 percent platinum hardened with 10 percent iridium. Pieces marked only PLAT or PT, or not marked at all, get tested to find the purity.

Before You Weigh

What Throws the Number Off

White gold that looks like platinum
White gold is gold with a bright plating, priced as gold. Use the gold calculator for anything stamped 14K or 18K.
Stones
Diamonds and other stones aren’t platinum. They come out of the metal weight and get valued on their own.
Mixed-metal findings
Earring posts, backs and some clasps on platinum pieces are made of white gold. They are priced as what they are.
Retail price
What a platinum ring cost new includes design, labor and markup. Melt value is the metal alone. More in our markup guide.

FAQ

Platinum Price Questions

Quick answers about platinum prices and melt value.

How much is a gram of platinum worth?
Divide the spot price per troy ounce by 31.1035. At $1,540 an ounce, pure platinum is about $49.51 a gram and PT950 about $47.04. The calculator above uses the live price.
What does PT950 mean?
95 percent platinum, usually alloyed with ruthenium, iridium or cobalt. PT900 is 90 percent and PT850 is 85 percent.
Is platinum worth more than gold?
Not always. They trade on separate markets and the gap between them moves. Compare today’s numbers here and on the gold page.
How can I tell platinum from white gold?
Check the stamp first: PT or PLAT for platinum, 14K or 18K for white gold. Platinum is also noticeably heavier for its size. A stamp is a claim, so we test every piece at the counter.
Why is my platinum ring worth less than I paid?
The retail price covered the design, the labor and the store’s margin. Melt value is only the metal, and stones are valued separately.
