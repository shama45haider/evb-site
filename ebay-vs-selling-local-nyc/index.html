eBay vs Selling Locally in NYC

Short answer: eBay reaches the most buyers, and for a lot of items it nets more than a cash offer, even after fees. The cost is time and risk: listing and shipping, payment holds for new sellers, and a buyer's right to return anything they say isn't as described. Time and Risk

Returns, Claims, Holds and Your Time

This is where selling on eBay costs more than the fee table shows.

“Not as described” returns
Under eBay's Money Back Guarantee, a buyer can return an item that doesn't match the listing even if you don't accept returns. They have up to 30 days after delivery to ask, and the seller pays return shipping.
Payment holds
New private sellers typically get funds 2 days after delivery with an eBay label, about 7 days after delivery with another label, and 31 days after payment if the item isn't tracked. High-priced items are typically held 31 days. (eBay payment holds)
Getting it to your bank
For established sellers, proceeds are typically available within 48 hours of the buyer's payment, then take up to 2 business days to clear at your bank, or about 30 minutes with a $2.00 express payout. (eBay getting paid)
Authentication
Sneakers from $100, handbags, jewelry and streetwear from $500, and watches from $2,000 go to an authenticator before the buyer gets them. It's free for the seller, but it adds days, and an item that fails isn't delivered and the buyer is refunded.
Disputes
Shipping something valuable to a stranger leaves room for a dispute: a buyer says the box arrived empty, or the item isn't the one in the photos. Tracking, detailed photos and signature on delivery help you make your case, but you're the one making it.
Your time
Photos, a description, questions from buyers, packing and a trip to ship it. For one item that's an evening. For a closet full, it's a project.

At our counter, we check the item in front of you and pay the same day. Once you're paid, the sale is done: no return window, no claim, no hold.

By Category

How It Plays Out for What We Buy

eBay's rate changes a lot from one category to the next, so the answer depends on what you're selling.

Sneakers

At 8% with no per-order fee from $150, eBay's published sneaker fee is lower than StockX's or GOAT's combined fees for a new seller. Pairs from $100 go through authentication, and eBay sends a free label to the authenticator. See the StockX and GOAT math.

Designer bags

15% up to $2,000 makes a mid-priced bag one of eBay's more expensive categories. Over $2,000 the rate drops to 9% of the whole sale. Bags from $500 are authenticated before delivery.

Jewelry, watches & gold

Jewelry is 15% up to $5,000. Watches step down from 15% to 6.5% above $1,000. For plain gold, melt value is public: check the gold price per gram before you compare any offer.

Electronics

Phones, laptops and cameras fall under the 13.6% rate. They're also where a “not working as described” claim is easiest to make, so test everything and describe it exactly. Sign out of your accounts and wipe it before any sale.

The Honest Answer

When eBay Is the Better Call

For items with a wide audience and a clear value, eBay's reach can be worth the fee and the wait.

  • The item is valuable enough that the gap between eBay's net and a cash offer matters to you
  • It's easy to photograph and describe exactly, which keeps “not as described” claims down
  • You already sell online, so you're past the new-seller holds
  • It's a niche piece whose collectors are spread across the country
  • You can wait weeks for the money and absorb a return

Sell Locally

When Selling Locally Makes More Sense

Sometimes a finished sale today is worth more than a bigger number later.

  • You need the money today
  • The item is expensive enough that a return or a dispute would really hurt
  • You're new to selling online and would face payment holds
  • You have things across categories, like sneakers, a bag, a phone and some gold, and want one trip
  • You'd rather trade toward something in our store

FAQ

eBay or Selling Locally?

What sellers ask us before they list something on eBay.

Is selling on eBay worth it?
For many items, yes, if you can wait and handle the shipping. After fees, eBay usually nets more than a cash offer. What you give up is speed and certainty: payment holds for new sellers, a 30-day window for not-as-described returns, and the time it takes to list and ship.
How much does eBay take when you sell?
For most categories, 13.6% of the total sale (including shipping and tax) up to $7,500, plus $0.40 per order. Sneakers of $150 or more are 8% with no per-order fee. Handbags and jewelry are 15% at lower prices. These rates are as published in November 2026.
Can a buyer return an item if I don't accept returns?
Yes, if they say it doesn't match the listing. Under eBay's Money Back Guarantee they can request a return up to 30 days after delivery, and the seller pays return shipping.
When does eBay pay sellers?
Proceeds are typically available within 48 hours of the buyer's payment, then take up to 2 business days to reach your bank. New private sellers typically wait until 2 days after delivery with an eBay label, and high-priced items are typically held 31 days.
Will a local shop pay what an item sells for on eBay?
Usually not. A shop buying for cash has to resell the item itself. Compare a cash offer with what eBay would net you after fees, shipping and the wait, not with the listing price.
What do I need to sell to you?
