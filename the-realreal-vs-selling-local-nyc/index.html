Time, Control, Risk

What Consignment Asks of You

The commission rate is the headline. These are the terms underneath it.

Waiting for the sale
Consignment pays when the item sells, not when you hand it over. A sought-after bag can go quickly; a less popular one can sit.
Who controls the price
The RealReal sets the starting price from current demand and past sales, and makes gradual price updates if the item doesn't sell. Your share is calculated on the final price after sitewide promotional discounts, excluding tax and shipping.
The payment calendar
The RealReal pays monthly, on the 15th, for the previous month's sales. An item that sells on the 2nd of a month is paid about six weeks later.
Lower-priced pieces
The RealReal's published rates start at 20% of the net selling price for items under $100. Consignment pays best on high-value pieces.
Your item travels
Online services work by mail or appointment, so your piece leaves your hands before you're paid. Fashionphile, for example, offers free insured shipping and pays buyouts after the item arrives and is authenticated.
Selling outright
We authenticate the piece in front of you, make an offer and pay the same day. No commission, no markdowns, no wait. The trade-off is the number: an outright offer is usually below what the piece eventually sells for on consignment.

Other Buyout Services

Fashionphile and Rebag Buy Outright Too

Not every online luxury service is consignment. Fashionphile's Sell To Us page makes buyout offers and pays up front: online offers arrive by email within 7 to 10 business days, and payment comes 2 to 4 days after your item arrives and is authenticated. At its Selling Studios, payment is made once the item is authenticated.

Rebag's Clair tool gives an instant offer for most styles, or a quote by email within 2 business days, and Rebag has started offering consignment as well.

A buyout from any of them works the way ours does: someone makes you an offer, pays you, and the item is theirs to resell. So get more than one offer. Ours takes a text: send photos to 917-608-8939 for a rough range, and we make the final offer in person at 39 Avenue A.

The Honest Answer

When Consignment Is the Better Call

If the piece is in demand and you can wait, consignment can put more in your pocket than any outright offer.

  • A rare or sought-after bag, fine jewelry or a luxury watch, where published rates are highest
  • You don't need the money for a month or two
  • You're fine with the platform setting and adjusting the price
  • You consign often, so rewards tiers raise your share

Sell Outright

When Selling Outright Makes More Sense

A fixed number today is worth more to some sellers than a possible bigger one later.

  • You need the money now, or want a set amount instead of a share of an unknown price
  • The piece is worn, or missing its box or dust bag
  • It's a lower-priced item, where consignment rates start much lower
  • You'd rather not ship a valuable piece
  • You want to trade toward something in our store

FAQ

The RealReal or Selling Outright?

What sellers ask us before they consign a bag, a watch or a piece of jewelry.

Is The RealReal worth it?
It can be, for the right piece. As published in November 2026, its rates reach 80% to 85% of the net selling price for fine jewelry, luxury watches and rare handbags, but start at 20% for items under $100. You also wait for the sale and are paid on the 15th of the following month. If you need money now or the item is lower-priced, selling outright is often the better fit.
How much does The RealReal take?
It depends on the item's category, its net sale price, your rewards tier and how you take payment. As published in November 2026, consignors earn from 20% of the net selling price for items under $100 up to 80% to 85% for fine jewelry, luxury watches and rare handbags, with bonuses at higher rewards tiers and for choosing site credit.
Who sets the price on The RealReal?
The RealReal does, using current demand and past sales. If an item doesn't sell right away, it makes gradual price updates. Your share is calculated on the final price after sitewide promotional discounts, excluding tax and shipping.
What's the difference between consignment and a buyout?
With consignment, you're paid a share after the item sells, and the price isn't settled until then. With a buyout, a buyer makes you an offer, pays you, and the item is theirs. We buy outright and pay the same day.
Will you pay what The RealReal would?
Usually not. An outright offer is typically below what a piece eventually sells for on consignment. What it gets you is a fixed number, paid today, with no fees, no markdowns and no wait.
Can I get a quote before I come in?
Yes. Text photos of the front, back, interior, hardware and any serial or date code to 917-608-8939 for a rough range. Final offers are made in person. Bring a valid government ID; New York law requires it for every sale.
