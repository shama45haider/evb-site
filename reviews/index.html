Why people keep coming back

Most of what people mention in reviews comes down to the same few things: we look at your item in front of you, we tell you what we actually see, and we pay the same day. There's no shipping your stuff off and waiting a week to hear a number. There's no pressure to accept an offer — if you don't like it, you take your things and go.

A lot of our reviews mention sneakers specifically, which makes sense: it's one of the fastest categories to evaluate and pay out on. But the same process applies whether you're bringing in gold, a watch, a designer bag, streetwear, or electronics.
