How It Works

How We Test and Price Your Gold, Right in Front of You

Nothing happens behind a curtain. This is the exact process we run on every piece, out loud, at the counter.

About 5 minutes at the counter
  1. 01

    Read the Hallmark

    We look for karat stamps like 10K, 14K, 585 or 750 and maker's marks. A stamp is a starting point, never the final word, because fakes get stamped too.

  2. 02

    Run an Acid Test

    A small streak on a test stone and a drop of karat-matched acid confirms the purity. You watch the reaction yourself.

  3. 03

    Confirm Electronically

    For coins, bars and pieces we don't want to mark, an electronic verifier reads the metal with zero damage.

  4. 04

    Weigh, Price & Pay

    We weigh in grams on a scale facing you, show you the math against live spot, and pay you before you leave.

FAQ

Selling Gold in NYC: Questions, Answered Straight

The questions we hear most, including the ones people are usually too polite to ask out loud.

Where can I sell gold in NYC?
Bring it to East Village Buyers at 39 Avenue A, between East 2nd and East 3rd Streets in Manhattan's East Village. Walk in during store hours, we test and weigh your gold at the counter in front of you, and you're paid in cash the same day. The F train to 2nd Avenue and the 6 to Astor Place are both a short walk.
Where can I sell gold near me for cash today?
If you're anywhere in Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens or the Bronx, you can bring gold to our counter at 39 Avenue A and leave with cash the same visit. No appointment, no mailing anything away, and no waiting on a check to clear.
Where is the best place to sell gold jewelry in Manhattan?
Look for a licensed dealer who tests your gold in front of you and prices against the live spot price rather than a fixed in-house rate. We do both, and we're licensed under NYC DCA #2070477. You're welcome to get other offers first and come back.
How much is my gold worth right now?
It comes down to weight in grams, karat purity and the live spot price. Divide spot by 31.1035 for the per-gram price of pure gold, multiply by the purity, then by the weight. Our gold price per gram calculator runs that math with today's spot price. For an exact number, bring the piece in and we'll weigh and test it.
What is 14K gold worth per gram?
14K gold is 58.3% pure, so it's worth about 58.3% of the per-gram spot price of pure gold. That percentage never changes, but the dollar figure moves with the market every day. Our per-gram calculator shows today's number.
What do gold stamps like 585, 750 and 916 mean?
They're purity marks in parts per thousand: 417 is 10K, 585 is 14K, 750 is 18K, 916 is 22K and 999 is 24K. GF, GP, HGE and RGP mean gold-filled or plated, not solid gold. A stamp is a starting point, so we always confirm it with a test. More in our gold stamps guide.
How much do you pay compared to melt value?
Our offer starts from melt value (weight times purity times live spot) less a margin that covers refining and market risk, and we show you each number at the counter so you can compare. Designer, signed and collectible pieces can be priced above melt.
Do you test my gold in front of me?
Every time, no exceptions. We run an acid test and an electronic reading right at the counter so you can watch the whole process before we talk numbers.
Do you buy broken gold jewelry?
Yes. A broken clasp, a bent ring or a single earring doesn't change how much gold is actually in the piece, so it doesn't change what we pay.
Do you buy dental gold?
Yes. Crowns, bridges and fillings are often high-karat gold and test just like anything else. Bring them in.
Do you buy gold coins and gold bars?
Yes. American Eagles, Buffalos, Krugerrands, Maple Leafs, Sovereigns, pre-1933 US gold, and refiner bars from makers like PAMP and Valcambi. Bullion is priced off spot; rare dates and collectible coins can earn a premium.
Do I need a receipt to sell gold?
No receipt required. We only care what the piece is made of, not where it came from. You will need a valid government-issued ID, which New York law requires for this kind of sale.
Do you buy gold-plated jewelry?
Gold-plated pieces have very little actual gold in them, so we usually pass. If you're not sure whether something is plated, solid or gold-filled, bring it in and we'll test it for free.
Is there a limit on how much gold you'll buy?
No. We buy everything from one broken chain to a full estate collection. For a large amount, text photos to 917-608-8939 ahead of time so we can set aside time to go through everything properly.
How fast do I get paid?
Same day. As soon as you accept our offer, you're paid before you leave the counter.
Do I need an appointment?
Walk-ins are always welcome. If you're bringing in a large or high-value collection, a heads-up text helps us prepare, though it's not required.
Are you licensed to buy gold in NYC?
Yes. NYC DCA license #2070477 (Vintage USA Inc, DBA East Village Buyers).
Can I text photos before I come in?
Yes. Text clear photos to 917-608-8939 for a rough idea. We'll still need to weigh and test the piece in person for a final number.
