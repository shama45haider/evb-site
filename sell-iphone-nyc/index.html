Pricing

What Moves an iPhone Offer

Two phones of the same model can be far apart in value. Here is what decides it.

Model and storage
The model and storage set the starting point. Settings > General > About shows both.
Carrier lock
Unlocked phones bring the most. Carrier-locked phones are case by case. A phone still being paid off usually stays locked to its carrier until the balance is cleared.
Battery health
Settings > Battery shows maximum capacity. Under 80% usually means the battery is due for replacement, and the offer reflects that. iPhone 15 and later also show the cycle count under Settings > General > About.
Screen
Cracks, dead pixels, lines, burn-in and touch problems lower the offer. An original Apple screen is worth more than a replacement.
Parts and service history
Settings > General > About lists replaced parts. A part marked Unknown is not a genuine Apple part, and it lowers the number.
Face ID and cameras
Face ID that stopped working after a drop or a repair, a cloudy camera lens and dead speakers or microphones all come off the offer.

Not sure which model you have? Settings > General > About shows the model name and storage. Text it with photos to 917-608-8939.

How We Check It

How We Test an iPhone

We test the phone at the counter with you there and explain anything that changes the offer.

  • Activation Lock: Find My off and the phone signed out of your Apple Account
  • IMEI status: whether the phone is blacklisted as lost, stolen or unpaid, and which carrier it's locked to
  • Battery health and, on newer models, cycle count
  • Parts and Service History for anything marked Unknown
  • Screen, touch, Face ID, cameras, speakers, microphones, buttons and charging port

Get Ready

Before You Bring It In

A phone with Activation Lock on can't be reset or used by anyone else, so turn off Find My before you come in. If Stolen Device Protection is on, turn it off first. Away from home or work that starts a security delay of about an hour, so do it before you leave the house.

  • Back up to iCloud or a computer
  • Move your eSIM or SIM to your new phone and check that it works
  • Turn off Stolen Device Protection, then Find My, and sign out of your Apple Account; we can help with the reset at the counter
  • If the phone is financed, check with your carrier that it's paid off
  • Bring a valid government-issued ID; New York law requires it for every sale

FAQ

iPhone Questions

What sellers ask us most at the counter.

Do you buy iCloud-locked iPhones?
No. A phone with Activation Lock on can't be reset or used by anyone else. If it's yours, turn off Find My on the phone, or remove it from your account at iCloud.com, before you come in.
Do you buy carrier-locked iPhones?
Case by case. Unlocked phones bring more. Once a phone is paid off, carriers will generally unlock it if you ask.
Do you buy iPhones with a cracked screen?
Yes. The offer is reduced by what the repair costs. A cracked back glass is handled the same way.
My iPhone says Unknown Part. Will you buy it?
It depends on the part. A non-Apple screen or battery lowers the offer, and we'll show you what the phone reports in Parts and Service History.
What about a financed or blacklisted phone?
Carrier status has to be clean. A phone reported lost or stolen, or blocked for an unpaid balance, is blocked by carriers, so we can't buy it. A financed phone that's paid off is fine.
Can I get a quote before coming in?
Yes. Text the model, storage, battery health percentage and photos of the screen and back to 917-608-8939 for a rough range. Final offers are made in person.
