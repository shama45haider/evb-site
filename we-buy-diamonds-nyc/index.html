How It Works

How We Grade and Test Your Diamond, Right in Front of You

Nothing leaves your sight. This is the process we run on every stone, out loud, at the counter.

Free to test, no obligation to sell
  1. 01

    Look Under the Loupe

    The stone stays on the counter where you can see it. We check it under a 10x loupe and say out loud what we’re seeing, including any chips or damage.

  2. 02

    Run the Diamond Testers

    Diamond and moissanite testers separate a real diamond from glass, cubic zirconia, white sapphire and moissanite. You watch every reading.

  3. 03

    Natural or Lab-Grown?

    Testers alone can’t tell natural from lab-grown, so we look closer with the loupe and UV light and read any grading report. Then we tell you honestly which one you have.

  4. 04

    Grade, Price & Pay

    We walk you through cut, color, clarity and carat weight, value the setting on its own, and explain the number. Accept it and you’re paid before you leave.

How We Price It

What Actually Decides Your Number

Diamonds don’t have a spot price the way gold and silver do. Two stones of the same carat weight can be worth wildly different amounts, so every piece has to be looked at on its own.

  • Carat Weight

    Matters, but not on a straight line. Value climbs sharply at the round numbers buyers ask for, so a stone just under 1.00ct is worth noticeably less than one just over.

  • Cut

    The one most people underestimate. A well-cut stone returns light far better than a poorly cut one of identical weight and color, and that difference is visible across the counter.

  • Color & Clarity

    Set the grade range. We’ll show you what we’re seeing under magnification rather than just naming a letter and expecting you to take our word for it.

  • Natural or Lab-Grown

    Changes the answer completely. Lab-grown stones resell for a fraction of a comparable natural diamond, and we test rather than assume — sometimes that means telling someone their stone isn’t what they were originally told it was.

  • The Setting

    Valued separately, on its gold or platinum content, then added to the stone value.

In Person vs. Mail-In

Why Not Just Mail It Somewhere?

Mail-in diamond buyers advertise heavily and the pitch sounds easy. Here’s the part that gets left out.

Mail-in buyer

You Ship It and Hope

You ship your ring to someone you’ve never met, wait, and then receive an offer once they already have the stone in hand. If the number comes back lower than you expected, your leverage at that point is essentially zero.

At our counter

It Never Leaves Your Sight

Selling in person removes that entire problem. You keep your ring the whole time, you watch the evaluation happen, and you can end the conversation and walk out with it at any point. If we’re not the right buyer for your piece, we’ll tell you that too.

FAQ

Selling a Diamond in NYC, Answered Straight

The questions we get asked at the counter most often, including the ones people feel awkward asking.

Where can I sell my diamond ring in NYC?
You can sell a diamond ring in person at East Village Buyers, 39 Avenue A in Manhattan's East Village. Walk in during store hours, we examine the stone in front of you, explain what we are seeing, and pay cash the same day if you accept the offer. No appointment needed and no shipping your ring off to a stranger.
Where can I sell a diamond without a GIA certificate?
Right here. A certificate makes the conversation faster, but it is not required. We grade the stone ourselves at the counter using a loupe and testing equipment, and walk you through the color, clarity, cut, and carat weight before we ever mention a number.
How much is my diamond ring actually worth?
It depends on the four Cs, carat weight, cut, color, and clarity, plus the setting metal and whether the stone is natural or lab-grown. Two rings that look identical across a counter can be worth very different amounts, which is why we examine each one individually instead of quoting from a photo.
Do you buy lab-grown diamonds?
We do, though it is worth knowing upfront that lab-grown stones resell for considerably less than natural diamonds of the same size and grade. We will tell you honestly which one you have, including in cases where the original seller was not clear about it.
Do you buy loose diamonds that are not set in jewelry?
Yes. Loose stones are actually easier to grade accurately since nothing is hidden by a setting. Bring them in however you have them stored.
Do I need the original receipt or box to sell a diamond?
No. Paperwork can help establish provenance on branded pieces like Tiffany or Cartier, but it is not required to sell. Bring a valid government-issued ID, which New York law requires for this kind of transaction.
Can I sell a diamond I inherited?
Yes, inherited jewelry is very common here. You do not need paperwork tracing it back, just a valid ID of your own. If it is part of a larger estate, text photos ahead so we can set aside enough time.
How fast do I get paid for a diamond?
Same day. Once you accept the offer, you are paid before you leave the counter, with no waiting on a wire or a check to clear.
Is it better to sell a diamond in person or online?
Online buyers require you to mail your ring away and wait for an offer from someone you cannot see, and a lowered counteroffer often arrives once they already have your stone. In person you keep possession the entire time and can walk away at any point.
Do you buy old European and Old Mine cut diamonds?
Yes, we see them every week in inherited and estate jewelry. Older cuts like European, Old Mine and transitional are graded differently than modern brilliants, and we price them on their own terms.
Does a broken or old-fashioned setting lower what my diamond is worth?
Not the diamond itself. A chipped setting or an out-of-style mount does not hurt the stone, and the stone is usually where most of the value sits. The setting is valued separately on its gold or platinum content.
Do you buy Tiffany, Cartier and other designer diamond jewelry?
Yes. Tiffany, Cartier, Van Cleef and similar houses carry a premium above stone value when the piece is genuine. Original boxes and papers help but are not required.
Is a diamond just under 1 carat worth less than a 1 carat stone?
Usually, yes. Diamond value does not climb on a straight line with carat weight. It climbs sharply at the round numbers buyers ask for, so a stone just under 1.00ct is worth noticeably less than one just over.

Yes. Text a photo to 917-608-8939 and we'll give you a rough read before you make the trip. A final number still needs the stone in hand, because two rings that look identical can be worth very different amounts.
What if I decide not to sell after you look at it?
That is a completely normal outcome and it costs you nothing. Plenty of people come in just to find out what they actually have.
Do I need an appointment to sell a diamond?
Walk-ins are welcome during store hours. For a high-value stone or a full collection, a quick text to 917-608-8939 ahead of time helps us set aside proper time for it.
